Quick guide - Processing officers

One box per task: the main steps and how you know it is done. For step-by-step screenshots, clips and troubleshooting, click the card code to open the task card on the guide site. Sign in at bo.vifc.gov.vn with your internal account. Button and menu names are written exactly as shown on screen.

Processing dossiers

CB.01See what needs your action

  1. In the left bar, click Request.
  2. Four counters at the top: Overdue, Due today, In Progress, Following.
  3. The In Progress panel lists each dossier with its status, sender and the DUE TO line.
  4. The My requests and Processed panels on the right show dossiers you created and dossiers you have handled.
  5. Click a dossier name in the In Progress panel to open it.
Done when: The dossier detail page opens.
Task card HD.CB.01 · Clip

CB.02Open and review a dossier

  1. The procedure name is at the top.
  2. Click a section, or click Next, to read what the applicant declared.
  3. Scroll down to the attachments section to see documents by group.
  4. At the bottom, the routing table shows which steps the dossier has passed, who handled it, when, and the action taken.
Done when: You have read every section and attachment and know which step the dossier is at.
Task card HD.CB.02 · Clip

CB.03Decide at your step

  1. The action buttons are on the top bar of the detail page.
  2. Write your remarks in the Note box below the form before clicking a button.
  3. Dossier complete: click Complete dossier.
  4. Dossier incomplete or incorrect: click Need more Info.
  5. Dossier not eligible: click Return.
  6. Not decided yet: click Save to keep what you wrote, or Exit to leave.
Done when: The routing table shows a new row with your action.
Task card HD.CB.03 · Clip

Tracking

CB.04Follow dossiers in Dossier management

  1. In the left bar, click Application (QL Hồ sơ in Vietnamese).
  2. Three counters at the top: to process, following, and processed.
  3. The dossier list shows each dossier with its deadline, progress and status.
  4. The recent-activity panel records who changed which dossier from which status to which.
  5. Under the dossier catalogue on the left, click ĐĂNG KÝ THÀNH VIÊN to open the member-registration group.
Done when: You can read the latest status of a dossier in the recent-activity panel.
Task card HD.CB.04 · Clip

Members and lookup

CB.05Add a member and issue its portal account

  1. Open the Thành viên module (crm.vifc.gov.vn).
  2. Click Thêm at the top right.
  3. In the THÀNH VIÊN form, enter Tên thành viên (required) and the contact details: short name, head office address, phone, Email chung.
  4. Click Lưu.
  5. Click the new enterprise name to reopen it and choose the Tài khoản tab.
  6. Click Thêm mới.
  7. The account shows in the table on the Tài khoản tab.
Done when: The enterprise can sign in to member.vifc.gov.vn with the new account.
Task card HD.CB.05 · Clip

CB.06Look up enterprise information

  1. In the left bar, click the enterprise lookup item, shown as Business l… (Tra cứu doanh nghiệp in Vietnamese).
  2. Enter the enterprise code in the search box.
  3. Click the search button to the right of the box.
  4. The quick-sample row offers a few sample codes.
Done when: The screen returns results for the code you entered.
Task card HD.CB.06 · Clip

CB.15Check for a duplicate enterprise name

  1. Open Request and click Check company name in the left list.
  2. Type the name in Proper Name (excluding enterprise type) (required).
  3. Optionally fill in Full Vietnamese Name, Abbreviated Name and Foreign Name to check them as well.
  4. Click Check company name and wait while the system queries the national database.
Done when: A result panel appears below the form.

Member registration flow

CB.07Receive a dossier, request a supplement

  1. Open QL Yêu Cầu (Request).
  2. Read the declared content using the TIẾN TRÌNH KÊ KHAI panel.
  3. Scroll to Ý kiến and Đính kèm to see the applicant's note and each document by group.
  4. If something is missing: click Bổ sung thông tin.
  5. Click the Bổ sung thông tin button at the top right of the dialog to send.
  6. If the dossier is complete: click Hồ sơ đầy đủ, then Tiếp tục in the Xác nhận dialog.
Done when: The dossier moves to your Đã xử lý panel and appears in QL Hồ sơ with the status Thẩm định.
Task card HD.CB.07 · Clip

CB.08Assign appraisal tasks

  1. Open QL Hồ sơ (Work).
  2. Click the Mô tả bước line to read what this stage requires.
  3. The Công việc con section lists the stage's sub-tasks, each at Chờ thực hiện.
  4. Inside the sub-task, click Chọn người xử lý.
  5. The assignee shows under the sub-task title.
Done when: Every sub-task has an assignee.
Task card HD.CB.08 · Clip

CB.09Do an assigned task and mark it done

  1. Open QL Hồ sơ.
  2. The sub-task screen has Đính kèm (attachments), Quy trình - Công việc liên quan and Công việc con.
  3. Click the + next to Đính kèm, choose Loại tài liệu, add a note, choose the file and click Upload.
  4. Click the status box at the top right and choose Đang thực hiện when you start.
  5. When finished, click the status box again and choose Đã hoàn tất.
Done when: On the dossier, the sub-task carries the label Đã hoàn tất with its completion time.
Task card HD.CB.09 · Clip

CB.10Issue the dossier receipt

  1. In the dossier at the Thẩm định stage, under Quy trình - Công việc liên quan, click PHIẾU TIẾP NHẬN HỒ SƠ.
  2. Enter Nội dung, then pick Ngày tiếp nhận hồ sơ and Ngày hẹn trả kết quả from the calendar (these two boxes do not accept typing).
  3. Click Tiếp nhận hồ sơ to save.
  4. Click Gửi.
  5. Click Đồng ý to send.
Done when: The receipt shows in the dossier under PHIẾU TIẾP NHẬN HỒ SƠ with its code and the label Phê duyệt.
Task card HD.CB.10 · Clip

CB.11Submit the approval decision

  1. When the sub-tasks are done, click the status box at the top right of the dossier and choose Ra quyết định.
  2. At the new stage, Quy trình - Công việc liên quan offers two forms.
  3. Scroll to Đính kèm and click Chọn files on the Báo cáo thẩm định line (required) to attach the appraisal report.
  4. Fill in Về việc, Căn cứ and Nội dung, then click Lưu and Gửi.
  5. In the Thông tin xử lý dialog, choose the approver for Trưởng / phó ban and Ban Chủ Tịch, add a comment if needed, and click Đồng ý.
Done when: On the dossier, the decision form carries the label Phê duyệt with the approval time, after the head of department agrees and the Presidium approves.
Task card HD.CB.11 · Clip

CB.12Issue the certificate and close the dossier

  1. Once the approval decision is approved, click the status box and choose Cấp giấy chứng nhận.
  2. Click Mô tả bước to read the stage's tasks: prepare the certificate, attach the file for the enterprise type, and move to Hoàn thành once it is approved.
  3. Quy trình - Công việc liên quan offers three forms: B2: KIỂM TRA VÀ XÁC NHẬN THÔNG TIN GIẤY CHỨNG NHẬN, THÔNG BÁO GIẤY CHỨNG NHẬN ĐẾN THÀNH VIÊN and TỜ TRÌNH GIẤY CHỨNG NHẬN.
  4. For form B2: choose Loại giấy chứng nhận, enter the company name, Thành phố (Tiếng anh) and Ngành nghề kinh doanh chính (required), click Xác nhận to save, then Gửi.
  5. When sending the Tờ trình, the Thông tin xử lý dialog lists the people at each step.
  6. Do sub-tasks B1 and B3.
Done when: The dossier shows the status Hoàn thành and a completion date at the bottom of the screen.
Task card HD.CB.12 · Clip

CB.13Approve a submitted form

  1. Open QL Yêu Cầu.
  2. For the full list, open Phê duyệt in the left bar and choose Chờ phê duyệt.
  3. Click a form name to open it.
  4. On a waiting form the toolbar shows the approval button: Đồng ý for heads and deputy heads, Phê duyệt for the Presidium.
  5. Scroll to the bottom: the Thông tin luân chuyển table records each step: handler, completion time, action and comment.
Done when: The form leaves Chờ phê duyệt, appears under Phê duyệt - Đã xử lý, and the routing table records your action.
